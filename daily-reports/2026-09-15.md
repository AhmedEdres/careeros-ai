## ⚠️ Methodology note — read before acting on this report

This is the **first run** of the automated daily search in this repo, so
there is nothing yet logged to cross-check against for duplicates.

More importantly: in today's run, **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) was
blocked network-wide in this execution environment** — not just for
Facebook (which was anticipated) but for *every* domain tested, including
LinkedIn, eJobs.ro, BestJobs.eu, OLX.ro, Jooble, Hipo.ro, Continental's and
Wipro's own careers sites, and even unrelated non-job sites used as a
control test. Only the search tool (which returns indexed snippets, not
live pages) was reachable.

Practical effect:
- I could **not open individual job pages** to confirm they are still
  live, read the full requirements, or pin down an exact posting date.
- For the handful of specific postings where the search index did surface
  a date, those dates were **months to over a year old** (e.g. a Wipro
  "Junior Buyer with English, Timișoara" listing dated 28 May 2025, an
  "Accounts Payable Officer with Italian" dated 21 Apr 2026, an "Accounts
  Payable Executive" dated 3 Jul 2025). That's strong evidence the search
  index is serving stale/cached snapshots of standing recruitment pages,
  not a live "posted in the last 3 days" feed.
- Given the instruction not to fabricate postings or invent dates, I am
  **not** presenting any of what I found today as verified "last 3 days"
  leads — I'd essentially be guessing on both freshness and continued
  validity, which isn't good enough to act on.

**Recommendation:** if this daily task is meant to keep working
automatically, the environment's network egress policy needs to allow
WebFetch to reach job-board and company-career domains (or the search
tool needs a way to return live, dated results). Worth flagging to
whoever configured this environment. In the meantime, the searches below
are still useful as a **map of which companies/sources are actively
recruiting for matching roles right now** — worth Ahmed checking directly.
